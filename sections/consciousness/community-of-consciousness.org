* Summary

*Topic overview (Daniel's notes not yet written)*

A community of consciousness refers to shared awareness among people, from collective representations to ideas of a shared mental sphere.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Émile Durkheim*: Sociologist (key thinker in the field); collective consciousness and The Elementary Forms of Religious Life (1912).
- *George Herbert Mead*: Philosopher and social psychologist; Mind, Self, and Society (1934).
- *Pierre Teilhard de Chardin*: Jesuit scientist and philosopher; The Phenomenon of Man and the "noosphere."
