Psychopharmacology studies how drugs affect mind and behavior, including research on psychedelics. This page is a neutral academic overview.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Albert Hofmann*: Chemist (key thinker in the field); first synthesized LSD and discovered its effects (1943).
- *Julius Axelrod*: Biochemist (key thinker in the field); neurotransmitter reuptake, Nobel Prize 1970.
- *Arvid Carlsson*: Pharmacologist (key thinker in the field); dopamine as a neurotransmitter, Nobel Prize 2000.
