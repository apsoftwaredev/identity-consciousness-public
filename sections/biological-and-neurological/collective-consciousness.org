* Summary

Collective consciousness refers to shared beliefs and awareness in a group, a term from sociology also used in psychology and philosophy.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Émile Durkheim*: Sociologist (key thinker in the field); collective consciousness and The Elementary Forms of Religious Life (1912).
- *Carl Jung*: Psychiatrist (key thinker in the field); analytical psychology, archetypes, the collective unconscious, and individuation.
- *Pierre Teilhard de Chardin*: Jesuit scientist and philosopher; The Phenomenon of Man and the "noosphere."
