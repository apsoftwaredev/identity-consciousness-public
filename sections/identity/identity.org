* Summary

*Topic overview (Daniel's notes not yet written)*

Identity asks what makes a person the same person over time and what makes each self distinct, drawing on philosophy, psychology, and culture.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *John Locke*: Philosopher; personal identity through consciousness and memory (Essay, 1694 edition); natural rights in Two Treatises of Government (1689).
- *David Hume*: Philosopher; A Treatise of Human Nature: the self as a bundle of perceptions, the passions (including pride and humility), and the problem of induction.
- *Derek Parfit*: Philosopher; Reasons and Persons (1984) on personal identity and what matters.
- *Paul Ricoeur*: Philosopher; Time and Narrative and Oneself as Another, on narrative identity.

Penseurs et philosophes essentiels :

- *Derek Parfit* : Philosophe ; Reasons and Persons (1984), sur l'identité personnelle et ce qui importe.
- *Paul Ricoeur* : Philosophe ; Temps et récit et Soi-même comme un autre, sur l'identité narrative.
