Humility, pride, and shame are self-evaluative attitudes and emotions, studied by moral philosophers and psychologists alike.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *David Hume*: Philosopher; A Treatise of Human Nature: the self as a bundle of perceptions, the passions (including pride and humility), and the problem of induction.
- *Aristotle*: Philosopher; Nicomachean Ethics (virtue as a mean), founder of formal logic, Metaphysics.
- *June Tangney*: Psychologist (key thinker in the field); research on shame and guilt.
