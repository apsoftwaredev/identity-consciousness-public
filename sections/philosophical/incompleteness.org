* Summary

*Topic overview (Daniel's notes not yet written)*

Incompleteness refers to Gödel's theorems, which show that consistent formal systems rich enough for arithmetic contain true statements they cannot prove.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Kurt Gödel*: Logician (key thinker in the field); the incompleteness theorems (1931).
- *Bertrand Russell*: Philosopher and logician; Principia Mathematica (with Whitehead) and logical analysis.
