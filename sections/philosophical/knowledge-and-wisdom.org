* Summary

Knowledge and wisdom are related but distinct: knowing facts versus knowing how to live and judge well.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Socrates*: Philosopher; the examined life, known through Plato's dialogues.
- *Aristotle*: Philosopher; Nicomachean Ethics (virtue as a mean), founder of formal logic, Metaphysics.
- *Michael Polanyi*: Scientist and philosopher; tacit knowledge, Personal Knowledge (1958).
