Being and nonbeing is one of the oldest questions in metaphysics: what it means for something to exist, or not.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Parmenides*: Pre-Socratic philosopher; the poem on what is and what is not.
- *Martin Heidegger*: Philosopher; Being and Time (1927) and the question of being.
- *Jean-Paul Sartre*: Philosopher; Being and Nothingness (1943), "existence precedes essence."
