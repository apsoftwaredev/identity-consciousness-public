The philosophical problems of quantum mechanics include measurement, interpretation, and what the theory says about reality.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Niels Bohr*: Physicist (key thinker in the field); complementarity and the Copenhagen interpretation.
- *Hugh Everett III*: Physicist (key thinker in the field); the relative-state, or many-worlds, interpretation (1957).
- *John Stewart Bell*: Physicist (key thinker in the field); Bell's theorem (1964) on quantum nonlocality.
