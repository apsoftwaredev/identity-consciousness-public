* Summary

*Topic overview (Daniel's notes not yet written)*

Eastern philosophy includes Indian, Chinese, Japanese, and other Asian traditions such as Vedanta, Buddhism, Confucianism, and Daoism.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Confucius*: Philosopher; the Analects, humaneness (ren) and ritual propriety (li).
- *Laozi*: Traditional author of the Tao Te Ching, founding text of Daoism.
- *Nagarjuna*: Buddhist philosopher; founder of Madhyamaka, the philosophy of emptiness.
- *Adi Shankara*: Philosopher; the classic teacher of Advaita Vedanta (non-dualism).
