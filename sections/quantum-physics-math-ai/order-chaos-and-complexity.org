Order, chaos, and complexity concern how simple rules produce complex, unpredictable, or self-organizing behavior.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Edward Lorenz*: Meteorologist (key thinker in the field); chaos theory and the "butterfly effect."
- *Ilya Prigogine*: Chemist (key thinker in the field); dissipative structures and self-organization, Nobel Prize 1977.
- *Stuart Kauffman*: Theoretical biologist (key thinker in the field); self-organization and complex systems.
