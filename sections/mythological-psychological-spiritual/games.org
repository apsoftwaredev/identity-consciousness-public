* Summary

*Topic overview (Daniel's notes not yet written)*

Games are rule-based play, studied in culture and in mathematics as game theory.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Johan Huizinga*: Historian; Homo Ludens (1938), on play in culture.
- *Roger Caillois*: Sociologist; Man, Play and Games (1958).
- *John von Neumann*: Mathematician (key thinker in the field); stored-program computer architecture and, with Oskar Morgenstern, game theory (1944).
