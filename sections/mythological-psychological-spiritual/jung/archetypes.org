In Jungian psychology, archetypes are universal patterns or images, such as the Shadow or the Self, said to arise from the collective unconscious.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Carl Jung*: Psychiatrist (key thinker in the field); analytical psychology, archetypes, the collective unconscious, and individuation.
- *Joseph Campbell*: Scholar of mythology; The Hero with a Thousand Faces (1949).
- *James Hillman*: Psychologist (key thinker in the field); founder of archetypal psychology.
