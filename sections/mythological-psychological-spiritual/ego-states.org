Ego states are organized patterns of thought, feeling, and behavior within one person, studied in transactional analysis and ego state therapy.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Eric Berne*: Psychiatrist (key thinker in the field); transactional analysis and the Parent, Adult, and Child ego states.
- *John and Helen Watkins*: Psychologists (key thinkers in the field); ego state therapy.
- *Ernest Hilgard*: Psychologist (key thinker in the field); neodissociation theory and the "hidden observer."
