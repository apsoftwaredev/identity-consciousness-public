Religion and spirituality cover belief, practice, experience, and community, studied by philosophy, psychology, and the history of religions.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *William James*: Psychologist and philosopher; "stream of consciousness," The Principles of Psychology (1890), The Varieties of Religious Experience (1902).
- *Mircea Eliade*: Historian of religions; The Sacred and the Profane (1957).
- *Rudolf Otto*: Theologian; The Idea of the Holy (1917) and the "numinous."
