* Summary

*From Daniel's notes, with a general topic overview*

This page holds a passage from Aquinas's commentary on Galatians 6, on bearing one another's burdens.

From Daniel's Roam notes: Daniel saved this passage from Thomas Aquinas, Commentary on Galatians, chapter 6, lecture 1, sections 347 and 348 ([aquinas.cc](https://aquinas.cc/la/en/~Gal.C6.L1.n347)). English translation as published there:

<quote>
<p />

347. Here he admonishes them to support one another, saying, bear one another's burdens. And this is to be done in three ways. In one way by patiently enduring the bodily or spiritual defects of another (Rom 15:1). In a second way by coming to one another's aid in their needs (Rom 12:13). In a third way by making satisfaction through prayers and works for the punishment one has incurred (Prov 18:19).
</quote>

<quote>
<p />

348. Now the reason for this admonition is the fulfillment of the law of Christ. But this is charity: the fulfillment of the law is love (Rom 13:10).
</quote>

Latin source text (quoted): alter alterius onera portate.

Résumé : Cette page contient un passage du commentaire de Thomas d'Aquin sur Galates 6, sur le fait de porter les fardeaux les uns des autres.

D'après les notes Roam de Daniel : Daniel a conservé ce passage de Thomas d'Aquin, Commentaire sur l'Épître aux Galates, chapitre 6, leçon 1, sections 347 et 348 ([aquinas.cc](https://aquinas.cc/la/en/~Gal.C6.L1.n347)). - *Thomas Aquinas* : Philosophe et théologien ; Somme théologique, unissant Aristote et la théologie chrétienne.
