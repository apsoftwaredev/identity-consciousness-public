** Summary

From Daniel's Roam notes: Daniel saved a passage from Aquinas's commentary on Galatians 6 (sections 347 and 348, from aquinas.cc) on bearing one another's burdens: by patiently enduring others' defects, by aiding one another in need, and by making satisfaction through prayer and good works. Aquinas explains that this fulfills the law of Christ, which is charity, and contrasts the old law of fear with the new law of love.
