* Summary

Thomas Aquinas was a thirteenth-century philosopher and theologian who joined Aristotelian philosophy with Christian theology.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Thomas Aquinas*: Philosopher and theologian; Summa Theologiae, joining Aristotle with Christian theology.
- *Aristotle*: Philosopher; Nicomachean Ethics (virtue as a mean), founder of formal logic, Metaphysics.
- *Augustine of Hippo*: Philosopher and theologian; Confessions, City of God, and evil as privation of good.
