* Summary

*Topic overview (Daniel's notes not yet written)*

Altered consciousness refers to states that differ from ordinary waking awareness, such as meditation, trance, and dreaming.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Charles Tart*: Psychologist (key thinker in the field); Altered States of Consciousness (1969).
- *William James*: Psychologist and philosopher; "stream of consciousness," The Principles of Psychology (1890), The Varieties of Religious Experience (1902).
- *Aldous Huxley*: Writer; The Perennial Philosophy (1945) and The Doors of Perception (1954).
