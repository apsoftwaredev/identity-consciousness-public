Existentialism is a philosophy centered on existence, freedom, and the search for meaning.

* Need-to-know philosophers and key thinkers

- *Søren Kierkegaard*: Philosopher; often called the father of existentialism.
- *Jean-Paul Sartre*: Philosopher; Being and Nothingness (1943), "existence precedes essence."
- *Albert Camus*: Writer and philosopher; The Myth of Sisyphus (1942) and the absurd.
